<p>
While I'm a year or two late, the <a href="https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/krissed" target="_blank">Krissed</a> meme is one of my favourites. I was turning over ideas in my head on what to carve for my friend Clark's pumpkin carving party, and I Krissed myself.
</p>
<p>
I donned my costume and headed to Clark's place with a line-art image of my reference ready. Immediately after Clark's party, I was also attending my friends Billy and Mikey's Halloween get together. It was a full night! And guess what, I met someone dressed as Kris Jenner at the second party.
</p>
<p>
There was a dilemma I faced once I took pumpkris home, for, nobody except my two nearest neighbors would be able to see her. This simply would not do. I got the idea to put her on my trunk and light a candle, so at least the people driving by would be able to enjoy the bizarreness of it all. 
</p>
<p>
And you know what happened, dear reader? One of my neighbors put <i>their</i> pumpkin on the hood of their truck! We had a little alley of jack-o'-lanterns in our apartment parking lot, even if it was just the two of us. I create culture.
</p>
