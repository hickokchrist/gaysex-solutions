<h2>Closing out July</h2>
<p>
I haven't been up to much since Pride weekend in the middle of July. Well, there is one thing. I'm calling myself the acting chair of the Merchandise Committee for the radio station at which I am a volunteer DJ. In reality though, I'm fully the merch chair. Right time and place, I suppose. We'll see how well I wade into the waters, but so far I've already been called a "shining star" by some folks with seniority. We just finished our fundraiser campaign, in which we exceeded our donation goal, and I get to coordinate fulfillment for all of the donor gifts and merchandise orders.
</p>
<div class="float-wrap">
<div class="img-item float-right">

![](/images/mugs.webp)
*We packed Demi Lovato's favorite dish - mugs - for our donors*

</div>

<p>
So far in my duties as merch chair, I've coordinated a sunday packing party in which some volunteers showed up to assemble boxes and package items. We wrote thank-you notes for each donor in the fundraiser, and I managed the logistics of keeping track of who received which gift and where their thank-you note is and all that good jazz. I had the board set up a dedicated email inbox for merchandising affairs, and was also able to work with a local print vendor to quote and purchase a quantity of t-shirts.
</p>

<p>
One decision I was proud to "champion" was whether to save approximately one dollar per shirt to use a different brand shirt blank than the past. Finally, I was able to have a direct hand in resisting the enshittification of <i>something</i>. The six percent or so savings wasn't nearly worth any decline in quality. So we're keeping the good shirts!
</p>

<p>
There's just the matter of confirming whether or not any orders since January have been fulfilled from our merchandise shop (where the heck did we leave off), and I'm basically ready to start shipping out. 
</p>
</div>

</p>
<p>
That night, MCR-T was playing at Holocene, I had snagged a ticket pretty much as soon as I heard. He was pretty cool, homie busted out a microphone and rapped live over his tracks.   <p>All the cool kids were at the Capitol Hill Block Party, but this broke bitch stayed home. One of my favorite new artists I discovered at <a href="/blog/2026-07-08" target="_blank">Beyond Wonderland</a>, <b>1991</b>, was playing at 45 East on Friday, August 7. I told my str8 bestie Kacey and he was down to visit for the weekend and have some adventures. We grabbed dinner at a birria food cart near me and headed to the club. The openers were really fun, they amped up the energy really good, but I was probably just excited to see the headliner. The show was amazing, so good. I've been listening to his EDC set online after stumbling into him at Beyond Wonderland.
  </p>
  <p>
  When I was at Beyond, the previous set had finished and I heard 1991 come on to the same stage my crew was leaving. They were going to the main stage to see an act I can't recall. I remember leaving and thinking... this guy (1991) is really good! I checked out the artist my friends went to see and didn't feel right, so I actually ditched them all and went back to see 1991 solo. I'm so glad I did! The way I describe it, his UK Drum 'n Bass sound woke my body up for the first time and made me feel feral - yet coordinated - while dancing in the crowd. I've been riding that high ever since. Anyway, that is why I was so excited to see 1991 again.
  </p>
  <p>
  During Friday's DJ set, I had a moment of ego validation as I stood in the crowd. I thought to myself, <i>"nobody knows that I played these same songs on the radio in this very city!"</i> Hole McJail walks among you, and I have big ideas for the future.
  </p>
  <p>
  It was really cool to share that experience with one of my best friends. I'd been telling him how much I've been wanting to go dancing for the past however many years. I was too scared to explore this world all on my own, shoutout my buddy Matt for showing me.
  </p>
