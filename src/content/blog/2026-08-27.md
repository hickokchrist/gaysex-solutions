<p>
That weekend, a friend across the creek from me was having their annual campout celebration. I made sure to join, this was the first year I'd actually stayed overnight. That Saturday I packed my stuff after finishing my latest episode of <a href="/sonic-stew" target="_blank">Sonic Stew</a> and went camping! I saw many friends for the first time since I'd deleted Facebook, it was a hoot and a holler! I brought glowsticks, some of the men had never seen one and tried to snap the whole thing in half lol oops... clarify your instructions, Hole!
</p>
<p>
After camping, I enjoyed the scene until noon when I was called to leave to attend a pool party that day. That was a lot of fun, a lovely time! But that, too, was cut short for a meeting I had to attend. Plus, the host invited me to the next pool party on my way out the door, I can't wait! At the meeting, I made a call-to-action for our DJs to hock our <a href="https://freeformpdx.square.site/" target="_blank">merch shop</a>. We've sold two cross-stitches of Jimothy the raccoon someone made and donated. My conversion rate alone is like 5% so far.
