<h2>The best month - August</h2>
<p>
By the time of this post, we're rolling around to the third saturday this August. It was a lot of fun, I met a new friend for dancing at Badlands, and we squished into sardine formation on the dance floor. It was very interesting being among all the straight girls, and I swear to god some people are bumping into others aggressively on purpose. After I tapped out, I got Baco-Tell on my way home and saw a live recreation of the <i>Creation of Adam</i> play out in front of me, except make it a drive-through.
</p>

<p>
Later that week, I enjoyed many dinners with friends. Honestly shoutout to everyone who feeds me, y'all are real ones 🤌 Thank you!
</p>
<p>
My pals Marc and Jon and I had dinner more than once. Jon is an excellent cook, he prepared ravioli one night with a delicious pesto sauce he made. Then another night we had soup and croque monsieurs made with turkey instead of ham. Absolutely amazing! That night, I had also replaced the cabin air filter in my car after ...too long a period of time had elapsed. After that was all said and done, we had dinner (thank you monsieur) and headed to a nearby place for some iced cream.
</p>

<p>
That weekend, a friend who lives across the quiet creek from me was having an annual campout celebration. I made sure to join, this was the first year I'd actually stayed overnight. That Saturday I packed my stuff after a busy morning finishing my latest episode of <a href="/sonic-stew" target="_blank">Sonic Stew</a> and went camping! I saw many friends for the first time since I'd deleted Facebook, it was a hoot and a holler! I brought glowsticks, some of the men had never seen one and tried to snap the whole thing in half lol oops... clarify your instructions, Hole!
</p>
<p>
Sunday morning, I enjoyed the scene until noon when I was called to leave to attend a pool party that day. That was a lot of fun, a lovely time! That event, too, was cut short, for a meeting I had to attend at FFP. While saying my goodbyes, the host invited me to the next pool party on my way out the door, I can't wait! At the meeting, I made a call-to-action for our DJs to hock our <a href="https://freeformpdx.square.site/" target="_blank">merch shop</a> in their shows. We've sold two cross-stitches of Jimothy the raccoon someone made and donated so far - my conversion rate alone is like 5%. 
</p>
