<h2>SPECTRAL ANALYSIS: It was actually Thursday</h2>
<p>
One thing I can say for certain, Rebecca Black chooses amazing opening acts. The last time I saw her, in 2025, she had Blue Hawaii opening. That group was a lot of fun, I added them to my rotation for sure. The same thing happened again!
</p>
<div class="img-item">

![](/images/bayli-wide.webp)
*BAYLI opened for Rebecca Black*

</div>
<h2>Stream "all of that" by BAYLI</h2>
<div class="float-wrap">
<div class="img-item float-right">

![](/images/bayli.webp)
*Stream BAYLI*
</div>
<p>
This night was a big event for me, because immediately after the concert ends, I would be driving to Seattle for a two-day rave. I had to make sure everything was taken care of before heading out to the show!
</p>
<p>
For this show, BAYLI opened the night and got the crowd amped up. I was listening to her music earlier that day and was super excited to see her opening performance.
</p>
<p>
There were definitely some fans of BAYLI in the crowd, I could hear them singing along to every word! Seeing her live was greatly enhanced by the energy of her fans in the crowd, I always love seeing a good opener with fans hyping them up in the audience.
</p>
</div>
<h2>Then go stream Rebecca Black's new album</h2>
<div class="float-wrap">
<div class="img-item float-left">

![](/images/hole-crowd-oct26.webp)
*I don't know these people*

<p>
At the show with me were some friends, but they had balcony tickets and I was on the floor. I saw a couple other friends in the crowd, but I'm not even kidding when I say they were not forward to the stage enough to my liking, so I kept walking forward into the crowd without stopping to say hi. Arriving late as I did, I didn't want to give up any space. As you can see from the images on this page, I didn't exactly end up close to the front.
</p>
<p>
I did end up running into a former roomate of mine and his crew in the spot I wound up in the crowd. We both refer to the house we shared in some shorthand way to reference that six dogs ruined the living situation. I call it the "dog house", I think his was something like the "dog domain". Can you imagine letting your dogs ... you know what, this is about Rebecca.
</p>

<p>
I've seen Rebecca Black live just once before, last year. Since last year, the quality and intensity of her performance is much higher. I'm a sucker for choreo, and you can tell she's been putting in the practice in the studio, and going hard. On top of that, she's literally been posting dance practice videos on her social media leading up to this album release. It's refreshing when you already like an artist, and they come back for their next cycle totally leveled up. 
</p>
<p>
It feels like I'm taking crazy pills when I tell people I'm a fan of Rebecca Black and they don't know she is an active pop star. Well, I suppose it's my job to put you on to some good stuff!
</p>

Side note, after the opener BAYLI finished her set, and before Rebecca came out, I'm pretty sure the PA system was playing 100% Britney Spears songs. I truly love getting to know an artist by the choices of songs they play in the interim between live performers. For example, Dev Lemons likes to play Death Grips.
<div class="img-item big-fill">

![](/images/bex-gif-1.gif)

</div>
The Portland crowd was going absolutely feral for Rebecca Black. I forgot my earplugs that day, and that was a huge mistake. There were a couple pauses after she'd finish a song where the crowd were just going absolutely ape over her. I think it might have made an impact on her, she made a point to mention (I think more than once?) how she feels so loved and welcomed by Portland. And I think she said or hinted that she can't wait to see us again!
</div>

My favourite song from the album came on, Waschesalon, and I was having a blast soaking it up. Her performance included an outfit change or two, and toward the end she brought out a chair for some chair choreography.
