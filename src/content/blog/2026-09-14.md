During the week I was sick, a lot of time was spent sleeping. It felt appropriate, there was little else I felt like doing. Fortunately, it was a very mild illness. Doubly-fortunately, I still have about 50 COVID tests stockpiled, free from the federal gov't in 2025. So I basically knew the exact moment I tested negative and could rejoin society.
<p>
There were a lot of events that week I missed out on, now that I look back. The last outdoor Tabor Dance, a friend's birthday, a pool party, the bridge city block party, new KFFP friends spinning live... At least I had been sick that week instead of the following week, because I ended up hosting four additional adult humans in my modest one-bedroom apartment for the weekend of 9/11.
</p>
<p>
My bestie Kacey, his wife Rachael, and their two buddies were traveling to Vancouver to see their boss compete in a natural bodybuilding competition all day on the 12th. We ended up at the Tom McCall Waterfront Park in Portland to see teams racing dragon-boats. We also checked out the Portland Saturday Market and got some baklava 🤤🍴
