<p>
The show started out strong, I arrived shortly after the opener, Killen, started his set. He was laying down some fun beats, the crowd was starting off slow, but people were excited to be there. The crowd was getting their drink on and loosening up over the course of Killen's opening set. Once his set ended, it seemed like jigitz was not ready to come on stage, so they played a song over the PA.
</p>
<div class="float-wrap">
<div class="img-item float-left">

![](/images/Jigitz.webp)
*jigitz looking pale in the bright light*

</div>
jigitz came out and the crowd exploded with energy. He played some bangers of his own, and I caught a Ninajirachi song and I think one of those millennial MGMT anthems in his setlist. The energy stayed high, the crowd was vibing, people were being pretty chill while jumping around having a good time. People had tutus in their hands and were waving them in the air, because, yknow, 50 Ballerinas tour. At the end, a girl wearing a tutu ended up behind the stage. I want to say she was one of us audience members and not part of the crew, just because she was alone and appeared only toward the end. Such a vibe.
<p>
The finale had a couple confetti cannons go off, that was a killer moment. jigitz kept wishing us goodnight and saying how much he loved Portland and kept playing another song, he did like five encores.
