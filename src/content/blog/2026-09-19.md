<h2>Slurping and gulping</h2>

<div class="img-item float-left">

<p>
Preparations for the show included baking some <a href="/gallery/polymer-flops" target="_blank">polymer clay beads</a> to make some kandi bracelets to hand out at the show. It's always so much fun to pore over the letters I have to see which words I can make. I'm quite proud of "GBBY DGLS", hopefully some Floptropicans were born.
</p>
<p>
I rolled up to the show solo and made my way to my comfort spot near the pillar where I stood to see Death Grips eleven years ago. Father Fannie was spinning tracks, and his track selection was really good. Looking back, I think I've seen him open for Cupcakke before, if not spinning during an event here or there I've attended. Father Fannie is a good time, he hyped us up really well, and I was really appreciating what he was doing on the speakers. Tinashe's Nasty X Slave 4 U was my favorite mashup Father Fannie played.
</p>

<p>
Mona Chrome came on stage to lip synch a few numbers and work the crowd. She was being the vibe and had a lot of good energy she gave to the audience. 
</p>
<p>
Cupcakke came out and we all started moaning and slurping and gulping out loud. She started with UFO and we all got sucked up by aliens in the sky. If you've been to a Cupcakke show, she likes to work the crowd in the same ways each time. She'll pass the microphone around and ask the audience for their best moans. She'll ask people to come up on stage to shake ass. One twink on stage allegedly turned 18 that day, who let their child listen to Cupcakke? 😜
</p>

<p>
Cupcakke handed the mic to one of the twinks on stage for the intro of one of her songs, I believe it was Sloppy Joe. Homie kept going into the first verse while Cupcakke looked at us like "I guess he's singing now?" He eventually noticed her tapping on him and asking for the mic - it was a pretty amusing moment. 
</p>
<p>
One of Cupcakke's hype guys came out to hype the crowd between songs and Cupcakke <i>immediately</i> told him to get off her stage lmao. Then Cupcakke was working the crowd and making deepthroat references, and was like, "let's do Deepthroat!" The DJ started playing One of My Bedbugs Ate My Pussy and she stayed on the mic being like "That's not right, change the song! stop the track!" until, like the first verse.
</p>
<p>
Cupcakke brought a crowd on stage to shake ass multiple times for different songs. Some little red riding hoods got up there, inspiring Cupcakke to play Little Red Riding Good next. On that note, let's talk about the outfits in the crowd! Cupcakke fans shows out, I need to work on my fit game. There was a duo dressed in full Squidward costumes. I stood near a trio of ladies dressed as cupcakes and desserts. 
</p>
